About the School

Abdul Rahim Shaheed High School is located in Kabul, Afghanistan. Public sources also refer to it as Abdul Rahim-e-Shaheed High School. A public reference lists 1970 as the school's founding year. The school is associated with the Dasht-e-Barchi area of western Kabul.

Important Dates

  1. 1970: The school is reported to have been founded.
  2. 2018: Teachers from the school received educational books.
  3. 19 April 2022: Explosions occurred at the school.
  4. 20 April 2022: The UN Security Council condemned the attack.
